# Al-Madeenah International College — Strategic Conclusions
### One page. The decisions, without the reasoning. Full reasoning in the Academic & Editorial Bible v1.0.

**What it is.** A distance-learning college, not a campus with a login screen — Arabic
language, Qur'an, and the Islamic sciences, taught to a published standard, to students
who cannot or choose not to relocate.

**The one non-negotiable.** Fixed academic standard, flexible pace. Progression is
gated by demonstrated mastery in front of a qualified human, never by time elapsed or
activities completed. A student who takes three years clears the same bar as one who
takes seven weeks.

**Four faculties, not more.** Arabic Language, Qur'an, Islamic Sciences — the core
academic ladder — plus a deliberately separate, lower-weight Professional & Specialist
Programmes stream (teacher training, corporate Arabic, seasonal intensives) that is
never presented as equal to the three-year core.

**Two routes through school-stage qualifications**, kept visibly distinct: an Academic
Route open now under the institution's own standard, and a Certificated School Route
aligned to an external examining body — not built or marketed until that body's
requirements are confirmed in writing.

**No ijāzah yet, stated plainly.** Deferred until named teachers with verified
transmission chains are genuinely on faculty. Every certificate states on its face
that it is not an ijāzah.

**Credentials never finance the institution.** First issue of every certificate and
transcript is free, permanently; verification is a free public utility, permanently.
Revenue comes from tuition, service tiers, the professional stream, and audited
sponsorship — never from the evidence of a student's own work.

**Regional pricing, disclosed, not hidden behind a VPN-defeatable IP check.** Currency
is independent of pricing band. A student's price is locked for their programme's
duration regardless of later band revisions.

**Regulated terms avoided by design, not by waiting for legal advice.** "University,"
"degree," "diploma," and "accredited" are not used anywhere. The entire award ladder —
Certificate, Certificate of Attainment, Certificate of Completion — was chosen
specifically to carry no such regulatory exposure from day one.

**Safeguarding is a gate, not a policy page.** No enrolment of anyone under 18 until a
named Safeguarding Lead, a jurisdiction-calibrated vetting standard, and a recording
policy are operative and published.

**Three digital experiences, plus one public utility, and nothing else.** Public
website · the Study (one unified learning environment, sign in once) · Executive.
Verification Register stands alone because it is a utility, not a portal. A fourth
portal requires a recorded, evidenced justification before it is built — not
accretion by habit.

**Royal means restraint, not gold foil.** Deep Royal Blue dominant; gold as a line or
mark, never a filled area; warm cream and ivory as the light foundation. No crescents,
minarets, or geometric star motifs required to signal "Islamic" — the discipline of the
palette and the seriousness of the content carry that instead.

**Arabic is authored, not translated.** Written in parallel by people thinking in each
language, from the first wireframe, not mirrored from English after the fact.

**Nine matters need the Founder, and only the Founder** — legal registration,
safeguarding appointments, the Statement of Academic Method, riwāyah confirmation,
mixed-gender policy, NBAIS recognition intent, a credential-register custodian, a
budget ceiling, and a trademark availability check. Full detail: Appendix A of the
Bible. Nothing else in this document is waiting on anyone.
