What Al-Madeenah currently is

Al-Madeenah International College for Arabic & Islamic Studies is a distance-learning institution, currently in its founding phase. By registering or using this website, you accept the following plainly-stated facts about our current status:

  • No cohort has yet begun studying, and no first-intake date is confirmed.
  • The College does not currently hold, and does not claim to hold, university status, degree-granting authority, or accreditation from any regulator.
  • No credential — certificate, ijāzah, or otherwise — is currently awardable. Registering reserves your place in the admissions queue; it is not an offer of a place.
  • Where a fact about the College is not yet true, we say so. A gap admitted here is the policy working as intended, not a defect in these Terms.

Eligibility and registration

Registration is currently open to applicants aged 16 and over. The College accepts students from age 10, but registration for anyone under 16 is not open yet, pending a published safeguarding structure. If you register, you confirm the information you provide — including your date of birth — is accurate. Providing a false date of birth to circumvent the age gate is a serious breach of these Terms and may result in your registration being revoked.

Your account

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity under your account. Tell us immediately at admissions.almadeenah@agentmail.to if you believe your account has been compromised. One account per person.

Acceptable use

You agree not to impersonate another person, submit false information, attempt unauthorised access to any part of the site or another person's account, harass or abuse any student, teacher or staff member, or use the site for any unlawful purpose. We may suspend or terminate an account for a clear breach of this section.

Fees

Registration is free. No payment is currently collected at any stage described on this site. If tuition fees or scholarship terms are introduced, they will be published clearly before any payment is requested — never assumed or auto-charged.

Changes to the service

Because the College is in its founding phase, the site and the programmes described on it will change materially as the institution develops. Where something material changes, we will say so, in the same place the original claim was made.

Limitation of liability

The site and registration process are provided on an "as is" basis during this founding phase. To the extent permitted by law, the College is not liable for indirect or consequential loss arising from your use of this site. Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Governing law

The College's legal registration and jurisdiction of incorporation is a founder-level decision not yet finalised and publicly recorded. Until it is, we do not state a governing law here rather than assert one we cannot yet substantiate; this section will be completed the moment that decision is made.
