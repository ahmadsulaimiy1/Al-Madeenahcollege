Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 15 August 2026

What we collect

When you register, we collect: your full name, email address, a password (which we hash with bcrypt on our server — we never see or store it in readable form), date of birth, country of residence, and your preferred study pace. Your date of birth is used specifically to apply the safeguarding age gate — registration for anyone under 16 is declined rather than accepted.

We do not currently run analytics, advertising trackers, or non-essential cookies on this site. If that changes, this section will say so plainly.

Why we collect it

  • To process and review your registration or application.
  • To verify age eligibility against the safeguarding gate.
  • To contact you about the status of your application and, once you are a student, about your studies.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, or safeguarding obligations that apply to the College.

We do not use your data to build an advertising profile, and we do not sell it.

How your password is handled

Your password is hashed with bcrypt the moment you submit the registration form, before it is written to the database. The plaintext is never stored, never logged, and never visible to anyone at the College. If you forget it, it is reset, never retrieved.

Where your data is stored

Registration data is stored in a Postgres database operated on the College's behalf by its infrastructure provider, with access restricted to those who administer this system.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other outside organisation, except where we are legally required to disclose it, or where sharing is necessary to protect the safety of a child under our safeguarding obligations.

How long we keep it

We retain registration data while your application is active or you remain a student, and for a reasonable period afterward. If your registration is declined or you ask us to delete your data, we remove it within 30 days unless a minimal record must be kept for safeguarding audit purposes.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to tell you what data we hold about you, correct it, delete it (subject to the exceptions above), or export it. Email admissions.almadeenah@agentmail.to from the address on your registration; we aim to respond within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Registration is currently open only to applicants aged 16 and over. The form is designed to decline, rather than silently accept, a date of birth indicating an applicant is under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last reviewed" date above will change with it. We will not make a material change that expands how existing data is used without saying so clearly here first.
