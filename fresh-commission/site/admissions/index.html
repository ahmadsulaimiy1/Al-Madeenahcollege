The journey

Five stages, in order.

Nothing before stage three asks for a payment or an account.

  1. Explore

    Read the three faculties, the four paces, and the fee bands — all before you have given us anything.

  2. Place yourself

    A free, untimed self-assessment suggests where to start. Calibration, not a test to pass.

  3. Apply

    One application, in one place, including a request for financial aid if you need it.

  4. Decision

    Every application receives an answer. A "not yet" is stated plainly, with the reason.

  5. Orientation

    A short, unhurried orientation to the Study before your first live class.

Stage two

The placement assessment.

It exists to answer the one question that quietly stops most people from ever applying anywhere: "where would I even start, and will I be embarrassed?"

Six statements about what you can currently do in Arabic, answered honestly, at your own pace.

What to expect

  • Free, no account required to begin
  • Untimed and resumable
  • Unlimited retakes
  • A suggested level, never a score

Stage three

Applying, honestly.

Institutional status

Applications for learners 16 and over are open. The College is in its founding phase — no cohort has yet begun, and this is the first intake.

Applying asks for: your name, date of birth, country of residence, preferred pace, and — optionally — whether you are requesting financial aid.

Status last reviewed: 12 August 2026

Pace

Four paces. One standard underneath all of them.

Flexible

— irregular

For unstable schedules, caregiving, or shift work.

Regular

4–6 h / week

The default pace.

Intensive

10–14 h / week

For a committed learner.

Accelerated

20–25 h / week

Near full-time study.

Who this is for

Admission is not narrowed to one kind of student.

  • Adults
  • Professionals
  • Teachers & trainee teachers
  • Converts to Islam
  • Families studying in parallel
  • Teenagers (16+, with guardian consent)

Enrolment under 16 is not yet open. A safeguarding structure — a named Safeguarding Lead, a vetting standard, and a recording policy — must be operative and published first.
