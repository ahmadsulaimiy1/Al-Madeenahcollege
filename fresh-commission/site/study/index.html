Listen, then repeat the construction aloud.

كِتَابُ الطَّالِبِ جَدِيدٌ

kitābu ṭ-ṭālibi jadīdun

The student's book is new. — kitāb (book) and al-ṭālib (the student) form an iḍāfah: the first word drops its article and takes no tanwīn.
