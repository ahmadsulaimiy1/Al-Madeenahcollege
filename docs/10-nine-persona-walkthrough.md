# The Nine-Persona Walkthrough — Walk 1

**6 August 2026 · `DX §13` · Cited as `PW-n`**
Al-Madinah International College of Arabic and Qur'anic Studies

The first walk of the shipped public site (20 pages, EN + AR) by the nine readers named in
`DX §13`. Each is recorded as **gets · doesn't get · verdict**.

**The method's value is that it finds absences, not flaws.** A beautiful page can fail a
reader completely by simply not answering their question, and no visual review will ever
catch that — the page looks finished. Every finding below is an absence unless marked
otherwise.

Findings are recorded here, not fixed silently. Each carries an owner: **fix now**,
**milestone**, or **blocked on the Founder**.

---

## 1 · Prospective parent, Nigeria

*The one thing they check first: **what does it cost?***

**Gets.** The fees page is reachable in one click from every page, has no email gate, and
opens by naming the problem in their terms — "one week's median income in London should not
cost six months' median income in Kano." Their band (D) is in the published table with the
countries listed by name. Six money pledges, each with the condition that would prove we
broke it. Scholarship is a section of one application, not a second process, and a
scholarship student is stated to be indistinguishable from a fee-paying one.

**Doesn't get.** **A number.** No tuition figure exists, and the page says so — but "index
0.15" against an unstated base answers nothing for a parent doing arithmetic on a phone.
They also do not learn what the money buys per week: how many contact hours, how many
teachers, how many students in the room at the Standard tier.

**Verdict.** ◐ **Honest, and not yet useful.** The honesty is correct and must not be traded
away. But the gap between "we publish everything" and "there is no number" is the single
widest credibility gap on the site, because it is the one claim a reader can test in ten
seconds and find empty.

> **PW-1 · fix now.** State on the fees page what a Band A price will be expressed *in* —
> per programme, per tenure, total, in the reader's currency — and what the reader will be
> able to compute the moment a figure exists. A worked example on a stated placeholder,
> clearly marked as arithmetic and not as a price, would close most of this.
> **PW-2 · blocked on the Founder.** The tuition figure itself (`D-11`).

## 2 · Prospective student, adult, working

*Can I fit this around my life?*

**Gets.** This reader is served better than any other. Three tenures are published with
weekly hour commitments on the face of the card (20–30, 8–12, self-paced), the open tenure
is stated to be a legitimate route rather than a lesser one, leave of absence carries no
penalty, price is locked for the duration, and progress is by mastery so falling behind the
calendar is not a failure state. Ramaḍān is an institutional operating mode with its own
timetable, not an interruption.

**Doesn't get.** What a week actually looks like. "8–12 hours" is a budget, not a schedule:
they cannot tell whether that is two evenings or six, whether classes are live at fixed
times in their timezone, or what happens if they miss one.

**Verdict.** ✅ **Strong.** The best-served reader on the site.

> **PW-3 · milestone.** A specimen week for each tenure — live contact, recorded study,
> revision, assessment — as a small timetable graphic. The `.path` component already
> exists and would carry it.

## 3 · Prospective student, GCC

*Is the Arabic real, and who teaches it?*

**Gets.** Arabic is authored in parallel, not translated, and every page has a complete
Arabic mirror at the same path — which this reader will notice within seconds and which
almost no competitor offers. Assessment across five modalities including classical text.
The College states it issues no fatwā and grants no ijāzah, and refers ijāzah-seeking
students onward.

**Doesn't get.** **Who teaches.** The faculty register is empty. This reader's entire
question is unanswered.

**Verdict.** ◐ **Credible in method, empty in people.** The empty register is the right
answer to give — it is checkable, and it will fill itself from the appointment record — but
it is still a "no" to the question actually asked.

> **PW-4 · blocked on the Founder.** The first academic appointment. Nothing on the site
> can substitute for it, and nothing should try.

## 4 · Donor / waqf contributor

*Where does the money go, and how do I give?*

**Gets.** Nothing. There is no giving surface of any kind.

**Doesn't get.** Everything: whether the College accepts donations, whether a waqf structure
exists, what a scholarship costs to fund, who receives it, what proportion of a gift reaches
a student, and whether zakāt is eligible (`D-11`, open).

**Verdict.** ✗ **Fails completely.** This is the largest single absence on the site, and it
is an absence of a *surface*, not of a fact — the scholarship model is already designed and
published; only the route to fund it is missing.

> **PW-5 · milestone.** A giving page: what a scholarship costs, what it funds, the
> proportion that reaches a student, and the zakāt position once `D-11` closes. Until
> then, one honest line on the fees page — that the College does not yet accept donations
> and why — is better than silence, because silence reads as concealment to this reader.

## 5 · Regulator

*Registration number and registering authority.*

**Gets.** A clear statement that the College is not accredited by any external
quality-assurance body, that its awards are institutional, and that the reader should
confirm they meet their requirements. A named-entity disclaimer distinguishing the College
from Al-Madinah International University (Malaysia) and from any body in Madinah.

**Doesn't get.** **A registered legal entity, a registration number, a registering
authority, or a registered address.** The site states these are not yet confirmed — which
is the correct disclosure — but this reader cannot proceed at all without them.

**Verdict.** ◐ **Correctly disclosed, structurally incomplete.** No design work can move
this.

> **PW-6 · blocked on the Founder.** The group facts: legal structure, registered name,
> registration standing. Already the first item in the README's "what the Founder needs to
> do next", and this walk raises its priority: it now blocks two personas, not one.

---

## 6 · Accreditation reviewer

*Curriculum, assessment criteria, QA outputs — without an account.*

**Gets.** The governance section states the principle exactly as this reader needs it: an
accreditation reviewer must be able to evaluate the institution without asking anyone's
permission, so nothing sits behind a login. The pedagogical sequence is published
(presentation → imitation → application → consolidation → demonstration), the four awards
each state what they attest to and how they are assessed, and the document register lists
six governing documents with versions and status.

**Doesn't get.** **The documents themselves.** The register says "not yet published here"
for all six. There is also no credit framework, no notional-learning-hours table on the
public site, and no level descriptors — so a reviewer cannot map any award to any
qualifications framework.

**Verdict.** ◐ **The best-structured failure on the site.** This reader is shown the shape
of everything they need and given none of the contents.

> **PW-7 · milestone.** Publish the documents. The register already names them; a document
> that is listed and withheld invites exactly the question the register was built to
> pre-empt.
> **PW-8 · critical, open (`A-1`).** The credit framework. Until it exists the institution
> is un-accreditable as drafted, and this persona cannot pass at any level of polish.

## 7 · Foreign university registrar

*Can I evaluate this transcript in under a minute?*

**Gets.** Four awards, each with what it attests to and how it was assessed. A public
verification route that requires no account. The explicit statement that no award is an
ijāzah and none confers a chain of transmission — which prevents the specific
misunderstanding this reader is most likely to have.

**Doesn't get.** Contact hours, level, credit value, or any mapping to a framework they
recognise. "Mastery of a named level" does not tell them which level. The award
nomenclature is also flagged on the site as awaiting legal advice (`D-03`), which is honest
but means the name on the transcript may not be the name here.

**Verdict.** ✗ **Cannot complete the task.** Same root cause as persona 6.

> **PW-9.** Duplicate of PW-8 — recorded separately because it fails a second reader for a
> second reason, which raises `A-1` above the other open decisions.

---

## 8 · Employer verifying a certificate

*Verification in under 30 seconds, no login.*

**Gets.** A verification page exists at a stable public path, is linked from the footer of
every page in both languages, and is outside the login. The register — not the paper — is
the source of truth, which is the correct architecture.

**Doesn't get.** A working lookup. Nothing to verify exists yet, and the page states this.
The reader also cannot see what a verification *result* looks like, so they cannot judge
whether it would satisfy them.

**Verdict.** ◐ **Right architecture, no specimen.**

> **PW-10 · fix now.** A specimen verification result on the verify page, clearly labelled
> as a specimen: what fields a real result will show, including the revocation state. This
> costs nothing, invents no institutional fact, and converts an empty page into a
> demonstration of method.

---

## 9 · Scholar assessing our seriousness

*Named faculty, chains, methodology, what we refuse to claim.*

**Gets.** More than any other reader, in the one dimension available. The refusals are
explicit and specific: no ijāzah until `مقرئون` with verified chains join the faculty; no
fatwā; recognised differences among the schools taught respectfully rather than
adjudicated; recitation assessed by ear by a qualified human, with machines permitted to
pre-screen and forbidden to certify; retention measured by unannounced re-testing rather
than pages reached; retakes free because charging for them would build an incentive to fail
students. The values table gives each value an operational test. The eight-value register
and the pedagogical sequence are both stated in Arabic in parallel.

**Doesn't get.** A named scholar, a riwāyah (`D-06`, open), or a statement of academic
method signed by anyone (`D-05`).

**Verdict.** ✅ **Strongest non-empty persona.** The refusals are doing the work that names
would otherwise do, and they are doing it well — this reader will recognise that a College
which publishes what it will not claim is more likely to be honest about what it does.

> **PW-11 · blocked on the Founder.** `D-05`, the Statement of Academic Method. It must
> come from the Founder and named scholars; it cannot be authored here.

**Two outright failures, five partial, two strong.**

The finding that matters most is that **six of the nine failures are absences of fact, not
of design** — they are blocked on the Founder or on `A-1`, and no amount of further visual
work will move them. Three are absences the build can close on its own:

| | Finding | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **PW-5** | A giving surface, or one honest line saying there is none | Small |
| **PW-10** | A specimen verification result | Small |
| **PW-1** | What a price will be expressed in, with worked arithmetic on a marked placeholder | Small |

Those three are the whole of what this walk asks the build to do, and they are worth more
than any further component. **This is the value of the method: three hours of design work
would have been spent on the wrong thing.**
