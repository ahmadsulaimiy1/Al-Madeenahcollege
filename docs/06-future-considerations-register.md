# Future Considerations Register

**Permanent · Opened 2 August 2026 · Cited as `F-nn`**

Ideas that are **valuable but premature**. Recorded here rather than forced into the
current phase — this register exists so that restraint costs us nothing in memory.

**How an item leaves this register:** its **trigger** becomes true, and the owning
authority (`EB §44.1`) moves it into a phase. Nothing leaves because someone wants it.

**How an item enters:** anyone may propose. It must state a trigger. **An item with no
trigger is not a future consideration — it is a wish, and wishes are not recorded here.**

Reviewed annually with the Editorial Bible (`EB §47`).
