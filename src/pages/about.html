IVision

To be the institution the world turns to when it wants Arabic and the Qur'an taught properly — and the proof that it can come from Africa.

We intend to become the most respected premium institution for Arabic language, Qur'anic memorisation and the Islamic sciences originating from the African continent, while remaining reachable by any sincere student in any economic circumstance.

That is stated as a destination we are travelling toward, not as an accomplished fact. No page here will claim a superlative — first, best, foremost — until an independent, nameable source says it first and we can cite them.

IIIOperating model

Online-first, by design.

Teaching is delivered online to students worldwide. That is a deliberate academic and access decision, not a placeholder for premises we do not have.

  1. 01Live classes with a named teacher, scheduled in the student's own timezone and around their prayer times.
  2. 02Recitation assessed by ear, by a qualified human. A machine may pre-screen; it never certifies.
  3. 03Scheduled مراجعة — revision is compulsory and system-scheduled, because retention is the standard, not pages reached.
  4. 04Offline study for every lesson, with the file size stated before download.
  5. 05Ramaḍān is an institutional operating mode with its own timetable, planned annually — not an interruption.

Institutional Status — Name

Al-Madinah International College of Arabic and Qur'anic Studies takes its name from the city that received the completion of the Qur'an and housed the first school in Islam. We have no institutional affiliation with any body in Madinah, Saudi Arabia, and we are unrelated to Al-Madinah International University (Malaysia).
