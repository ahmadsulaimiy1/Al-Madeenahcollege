01 · Regional Access Pricing

How your band is decided.

Your band is set by your billing country or declared country of residence — never silently by IP address. It is always displayed, always changeable in one click before any account exists, and verified at payment against your billing address.

Bands are assigned using published, citable data — World Bank income classification and PPP conversion factors — reviewed annually with the review date shown. There are no arbitrary differences.

A cheaper band never buys a lesser certificate. Tier buys service level. Band buys price level. Neither ever changes the academic standard.

02 · What is always true

Six commitments on money.

  1. 01Every price is published. No "contact us for pricing" on any standard programme.
  2. 02The published price is the price. No fee appears at checkout that was not visible before it.
  3. 03Total cost is always shown alongside any monthly figure. Never a monthly teaser alone.
  4. 04Instalments carry no penalty and are standard, not a concession.
  5. 05Your price is locked for the duration of an enrolled programme, whatever happens to bands or exchange rates.
  6. 06Certificates cost nothing. Production, first replacement and verification are included at every band and every tier.
03 · Scholarships

One application, whether or not you need help paying.

Financial aid is a section of the admissions form, never a second process. Two forms would mean the students least able to pay do the most work.

A scholarship student is indistinguishable from a fee-paying one — on every surface, to every teacher, and in every record a peer can see. There is no marker in the portal, none in the classroom, and none on the certificate. Dignity is the point.
