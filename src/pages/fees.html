01

IRegional Access Pricing

How your band is decided.

Your band is set by your billing country or declared country of residence — never silently by IP address. It is always displayed, always changeable in one click before any account exists, and verified at payment against your billing address.

Bands are assigned using published, citable data — World Bank income classification and PPP conversion factors — reviewed annually with the review date shown. There are no arbitrary differences.

A cheaper band never buys a lesser certificate. Tier buys service level. Band buys price level. Neither ever changes the academic standard.

What the figure will be, when there is one

Saying "there is no price" without saying what shape the price will take answers nothing for someone doing arithmetic on a phone. So: one figure per programme, per tenure, as a full total — not per month, not per module, not per hour. Your band multiplies that total. The arithmetic is the whole of it:

02

IIWhat is always true

Six commitments on money.

A promise nobody can test is decoration. Each of these states the condition under which you could say we broke it.

01

Every price is published.

No "contact us for pricing" on any standard programme, in any band, in any currency.

Broken if — any standard programme shows a price only on request
02

The published price is the price.

No fee appears at checkout that was not visible before it — no processing charge, no registration charge, no examination charge.

Broken if — your total at checkout exceeds the published total
03

Total cost is always shown.

Any monthly figure is displayed alongside the full programme total, at the same size. Never a monthly teaser alone.

Broken if — a monthly figure appears without its total beside it
04

Instalments carry no penalty.

Paying across the year costs exactly what paying at once costs. Instalments are standard, not a concession granted on request.

Broken if — the instalment total is higher than the single payment
05

Your price is locked.

For the full duration of an enrolled programme, whatever happens to the bands, to the exchange rate, or to our costs.

Broken if — an enrolled student is ever billed above their enrolment price
06

Certificates cost nothing.

Production, first replacement and verification are included at every band and every tier — including the scholarship band.

Broken if — any certificate, replacement or verification is ever charged for
03

IIIScholarships

One application, whether or not you need help paying.

Financial aid is a section of the admissions form, never a second process. Two forms would mean the students least able to pay do the most work.

A scholarship student is indistinguishable from a fee-paying one — on every surface, to every teacher, and in every record a peer can see. There is no marker in the portal, none in the classroom, and none on the certificate. Dignity is the point.

04

IVGiving

We do not yet accept donations.

There is no giving page, no waqf structure and no donation route, and we would rather tell you that plainly than leave you looking for one.

Taking money for scholarships before a registered entity exists to receive it, before a student exists to receive the scholarship, and before anyone can audit where it went, would be the wrong order to do things in.

Institutional Status — Giving

When giving opens it will publish, at minimum: what one scholarship costs, what proportion of a gift reaches a student, who receives it, and the College's stated position on زكاة eligibility — which is an open question awaiting scholarly advice, not one we will answer ourselves.
