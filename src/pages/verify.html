Verify a certificate

The paper is not the credential. The register is. A certificate is a receipt for a register entry — so checking it is free, instant and requires no account.

Institutional Status — Register

The College has issued no certificates, so the register is empty. When it is live it will run on its own host with its own uptime obligation, because a graduate may present a certificate in sixty years and it must still answer.

It will show only what a verifier needs: holder, award, level, date, and status — never a date of birth, address, photograph, marks or fees. A revoked certificate stays publicly visible as revoked.
