ITenure

Choose your tenure,
not your ceiling.

Everyone meets the same standard. What differs is how much of the week you can give it — so we publish tenures rather than a single duration, and a student on any tenure earns the identical certificate.

A term does not promote a student. An assessor does. There is no time limit on reaching a gate, retakes are free and unlimited, and a student who takes three years has still arrived.

On retakesCharging for retakes would build an incentive to fail students. We will not build that incentive — which is why the fee schedule can never contain one.

IIIThe standard

We count what stays.

A certificate is only worth the retesting behind it.

Editorial Bible §33 — mastery, not the calendar

Most academies record what a student reached. We record what they retained — a portion counts as held only once it survives unannounced re-testing after a defined interval.

That single choice makes our numbers smaller and our certificates worth more. It is also why scheduled مراجعة is compulsory rather than encouraged: revision is the discipline that makes memorisation permanent.

Recitation is assessed by ear, by a qualified human. A machine may pre-screen and flag. It never certifies.

Institutional Status

Al-Madinah International College is not currently accredited by any external quality-assurance body, has admitted no students, and has issued no certificates. It does not grant ijāzah and will not until qualified مقرئون with verifiable chains join the faculty.

Named faculty, a registered address, an academic calendar and a first-cohort date do not yet exist and are not shown anywhere on this site as though they did.
