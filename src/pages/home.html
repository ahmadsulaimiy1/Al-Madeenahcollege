01 · What decides progress

Mastery, not the calendar.

A term does not promote a student. An assessor does. Every level has a published mastery gate, and a student advances when they can demonstrate it — whether that takes eleven weeks or three years.

That single decision is what makes genuinely different durations coherent rather than a marketing claim. Four routes run through one standard.

03 · How we price

Published, not personalised.

A programme that costs one week's median income in London should not cost six months' median income in Kano. So we price by country — and we publish the table, the method, and the reasoning.

Your band is set by your billing country or declared country of residence — never silently by IP address. It is always shown, always changeable, and verified at payment. A cheaper band never buys a lesser certificate.

04 · What we do not claim

The honest position.

In a field full of unverifiable claims, the most useful thing we can publish is what is not yet true.

Institutional Status

Al-Madinah International College is not currently accredited by any external quality-assurance body, has no enrolled students, and has issued no certificates. It does not grant ijāzah and will not until qualified مقرئون with verifiable chains join the faculty.

Named faculty, a registered address, an academic calendar and a first-cohort date do not yet exist and are not shown anywhere on this site as though they did.

Last reviewed: 2 August 2026

We publish limitations in the same voice as everything else — no smaller type, no footnote. An institution that states its gaps plainly is more believable about everything else.
