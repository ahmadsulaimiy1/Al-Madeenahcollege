01 · The journey

Five steps, and what each is for.

  1. 01Read. Programmes, fees and the assessment criteria — all public, no account.
  2. 02Place yourself. A free, untimed self-assessment suggests your starting level. It is calibration, not a test to pass.
  3. 03Meet a teacher. One free trial lesson with a real member of faculty, after the assessment.
  4. 04Apply. One application, including financial aid if you need it — never a second process.
  5. 05Decision. Every application is answered. There are no silent rejections.

A signed status link is emailed when you submit, so you can check where your application stands at any hour without creating an account or contacting anyone.

02 · Placement

The placement assessment.

Untimed. Resumable. Unlimited retakes. Your progress saves as you go, and no score is ever shown to you as a grade.

It exists to answer the question that stops most people applying: "where would I even start, and will I be embarrassed?"

This is deliberately not gated behind an account. Gating our most useful instrument behind a signup would be self-defeating.
