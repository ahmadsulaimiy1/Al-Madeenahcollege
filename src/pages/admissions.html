01

IThe journey

Five steps, and what each is for.

Nothing in the first three steps requires an account, a payment, or an email address.

  1. Step one

    Read

    Programmes, fees and the assessment criteria — all public, no account.

  2. Step two

    Place yourself

    A free, untimed self-assessment suggests your starting level. Calibration, not a test to pass.

  3. Step three

    Meet a teacher

    One free trial lesson with a real member of faculty, after the assessment.

  4. Step four

    Apply

    One application, including financial aid if you need it — never a second process.

  5. Step five

    Decision

    Every application is answered. There are no silent rejections.

A signed status link is emailed when you submit, so you can check where your application stands at any hour without creating an account or contacting anyone.

02

IIPlacement

The placement assessment.

Untimed. Resumable. Unlimited retakes. Your progress saves as you go, and no score is ever shown to you as a grade.

It exists to answer the question that stops most people applying: "where would I even start, and will I be embarrassed?"

This is deliberately not gated behind an account. Gating our most useful instrument behind a signup would be self-defeating.
