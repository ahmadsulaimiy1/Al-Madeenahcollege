Programmes

01 · The four schools

School of Arabic Language

كلية اللغة العربية

Arabic taught as the instrument that opens the tradition — not as grammar drills, and not as phrasebook conversation. Assessed across five modalities including comprehension of classical text, which is where conversational programmes have nothing to offer.

  • Reading
  • Listening
  • Speaking
  • Writing
  • Classical text

School of Qur'an

كلية القرآن

Memorisation measured in itqān and retention, not pages reached. A portion counts as held only if it survives unannounced re-testing after a defined interval. Most academies count what was reached; we count what is retained.

  • Ḥifẓ
  • Tajwīd
  • Murājaʿah
  • Recitation assessment

School of Islamic Sciences

كلية العلوم الشرعية

The classical disciplines, taught from named texts, with recognised differences among the schools taught respectfully rather than adjudicated. The College issues no fatwā.

Institute of Teacher Certification

معهد إعداد المعلمين

Preparation for those who will teach Arabic and Qur'an to others — pedagogy, assessment, and the adab of the teaching relationship.

02 · How progression works

A gate, not a grade.

To advance, a student demonstrates mastery to a qualified human assessor. Not completes the lessons. Not passes a quiz. Demonstrates.

Gates are published in advance, so every student knows exactly what they must show. A failed gate is not a failure — it produces a specific diagnostic and a targeted remediation plan.

Retakes are unlimited and free, and there is no time limit on reaching a gate. A student who takes three years has still reached it.

Institutional Status — Ijāzah and award names

The College does not grant ijāzah. Every award states on its face that it is not an ijāzah and confers no chain of transmission. Students seeking one are referred onward. Ijāzah programmes begin only when مقرئون holding a verified chain join the faculty.

The Arabic award is shown here under a working name. "Diploma" is a regulated term in several markets and the final name awaits legal advice — so it is not printed here as though it were settled.
